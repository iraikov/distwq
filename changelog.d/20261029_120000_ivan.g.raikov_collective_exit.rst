Fixed
-----

- Collective mode no longer hangs at exit (issue 26).  When told to exit,
  each collective worker disconnected the group communicator, but its
  broker did not.  Disconnecting is collective, so the workers waited
  forever, and the controller and brokers blocked in ``MPI_Finalize``.
  Now the broker and its workers leave the group together.  A spawned
  group frees its merged communicator and disconnects the
  intercommunicator from ``MPI_Comm_spawn``, because under Open MPI
  disconnecting a merged communicator that spans two jobs does not
  complete.  A split group disconnects its merged communicator, as
  before, with the broker now taking part.
